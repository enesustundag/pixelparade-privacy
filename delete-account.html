Delete your account

Pixel Parade: Color Blast for Android and iOS
Developer: Kruxgen
Last updated: November 23, 2026

The Game creates a guest account on our server so your progress can be saved to the cloud. You can delete this account and its data at any time, either inside the Game or by email. Deletion is permanent: your progress cannot be restored afterwards.

Option 1: Delete in the Game (fastest)

  1. Open Pixel Parade and tap the Settings (gear) button.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Read what will be deleted and tap Continue.
  4. Type DELETE (or the word shown in your language) and tap Delete Forever.

Your account is deleted from our server right away, the data on your device is cleared and the Game starts fresh. An internet connection is required.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you no longer have the Game installed, or the in-game option does not work for you, email ugur.enes.ustundag@gmail.com with the subject "Pixel Parade account deletion request". Please include your player ID if you have it (Settings → tap the ID line to copy it). If you do not have it, tell us your device model and roughly when you last played, and we will help you find the account. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Analytics events already sent to Google Firebase are kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. On request (Option 2) we also ask Google to delete the analytics data linked to your player ID.
  • Crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own policies. Our purchase check records (product, transaction ID, time) may be kept for accounting and fraud prevention, and we keep records we are legally required to keep.
  • Ad data held by Google AdMob is managed under Google's own policy.

Purchases: one-time purchases such as Remove Ads belong to your Google Play or App Store account, not to your game account. After deleting your account you can get them back at any time with Settings → Restore Purchases. Coins and other consumable items are part of your progress and are deleted with it.

Request a copy of your data

To ask what data we hold about you, or for a copy of it, use Settings → Request My Data in the Game, or email the address above with the subject "Pixel Parade data request".
