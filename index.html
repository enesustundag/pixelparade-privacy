Privacy Policy

Pixel Parade: Color Blast · Developer: Kruxgen · Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Pixel Parade: Color Blast ("the Game") collects, why, and the choices you have. The Game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or other personal files.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (level, coins, lives, boosters, settings and purchases you own) is saved only on your device. A random player ID is created on first launch so that we can help you if you contact support. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The Game uses the following Google services. They may collect device-level information as described in their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions. Used to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. How Google uses data in ads.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics): app instance ID, device and app information, approximate country, and gameplay events such as level start, level win, level fail and purchases. Used to understand how the Game is played and to improve difficulty and balance. Firebase privacy.
  • Firebase Remote Config: used to adjust game settings (for example rewards) without an app update.
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases): payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers listed above.

3. Consent and your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google's certified consent tool). You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. Purchasing "Remove Ads" removes ads shown between levels.

4. Internet connection

The Game requires an internet connection to play. This is needed to deliver ads and in-app purchases.

5. Children

The Game's content is suitable for everyone, but the Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game. Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for the retention period set in our Firebase project (at most 14 months). To request deletion of analytics data linked to your player ID, email us with the player ID shown in Settings.

7. Security

Data sent to the services above is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the Game or on its store page.
