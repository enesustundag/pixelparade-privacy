Privacy Policy

Pixel Parade: Color Blast (the "Game") for Android and iOS
Developer: Kruxgen ("we", "us")
Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the Game collects, why, which service providers process it, and how you can ask us to delete it. The Game requires an internet connection.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location. You can choose a display name for your profile; it is shown to other players.
  • The Game creates a random player ID and a guest game account on our server so your progress can be saved to the cloud.
  • We use Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging) to understand how the Game is played, fix crashes, tune the Game and send optional notifications.
  • We use Google AdMob to show ads, with a consent message for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and Apple's tracking permission on iOS.
  • We use the Meta SDK only to measure our ads on Facebook and Instagram (see Meta advertising measurement).
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can delete your account in the Game (Settings → Delete Account), get a copy of your data (Settings → Request My Data), or ask us by email (see section 8).

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (level, coins, lives, boosters, town, album, settings and owned purchases) is stored on your device. On first launch the Game also creates a random player ID and a random login ID. These are generated by the Game; they are not your device's hardware ID or your advertising ID. The player ID is shown in Settings (with a Copy button) so you can quote it when you contact us. Uninstalling the Game removes the data stored on your device.

3. Our game server (cloud save and online features)

The Game connects to our own game server at nakama.struklabs.com, which runs the open-source Nakama game server software on cloud infrastructure we rent. Traffic to the server passes through Cloudflare, which acts as our network provider. On first launch the Game signs in to a guest account using the random login ID; no sign-up is needed. The server stores:

  • Account: your player ID, the random login ID, and, if you sign in, the link to your Google Play Games or Apple Game Center player ID (see section 4).
  • Cloud save: a copy of your game progress, so it can be restored on a new device or after reinstalling.
  • Profile: the display name you choose (3 to 16 letters or numbers, checked by a filter for offensive or official-looking words), the avatar and the frame you pick. If you do not choose a name, the Game uses an automatically generated nickname (for example an adjective, an animal and a number). There is no chat and no way to send messages to other players.
  • Pixel Arena and Parade League: your name, avatar, rank and score are shown to the other players in your group (up to 50 players).
  • Monthly Leaderboard: your name, avatar, score and a two-letter country code are shown to all players in the world and country rankings. The country code comes from your device's region setting, not from your IP address or GPS. The top 3 players of each month are kept in the Hall of Fame.
  • Gifts: gifts we send to your inbox and whether you have claimed them.
  • Performance data: device model, graphics chip, processor, memory size, screen size and refresh rate, operating system version, graphics settings, frame rate, device temperature status and memory use for your last 10 levels. We use this to find and fix slowdowns and overheating.
  • Level updates: the Game downloads new levels from this server.
  • Feedback: if you send us feedback in the game (Settings → Help → Send Feedback), we store your message, the topic you picked, your player ID, game version, language and device model on our server so our team can read it. We use it only to improve the game and answer you. You can send up to 5 messages a day.
  • Push notifications: if notifications are on, we store your device's notification token and game language on our server so we can send you game news and event reminders. Turning notifications off in Settings deletes it.
  • Purchases: when you buy something, your store receipt is sent to our server and checked with Google Play or Apple to confirm the purchase. We keep the product, transaction ID and time.

Like any internet service, the server and Cloudflare receive your IP address when the Game connects. Only you (through the Game) and we can read your saved data.

4. Google Play Games and Apple Game Center

On Android the Game signs in to Google Play Games; on iOS it signs in to Apple Game Center, if you are signed in on your device. This is used only to connect your progress to your Google or Game Center account, so you get it back on a new device. Our server receives a one-time sign-in code (Android) or a signed verification message (iOS), checks it with Google or Apple, and stores only your Play Games or Game Center player ID. We do not receive your email address or contacts. Google's and Apple's own privacy policies apply to these services.

6. Notifications

Reminders (for example "your lives are full", "your daily reward is ready" or "an event ends tomorrow") are scheduled on your device and are not sent to us. Push notifications are sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging. The Game first explains notifications in its own window (for example after you claim a daily reward) and shows your device's permission prompt only if you agree. On iPhone, a running event you have joined may also be shown on the Lock Screen (Live Activity); this is created on your device and is turned off together with the Game's notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in the Game's Settings or in your device settings; turning them off in the Game also unsubscribes you from push topics.

7. Your choices about ads and tracking

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Settings. If you do not consent, ad-related data is not used for personalization and Firebase Analytics is told not to use data for advertising, and the Meta SDK does not start.
  • iOS: the Game asks for Apple's tracking permission. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared with us, AdMob or Meta. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. The "No Ads" purchase removes ads shown between levels.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and its data yourself in the Game: Settings → Delete Account. Your account is deleted from our server right away (including your profile name and avatar, cloud save, Arena, League and Leaderboard entries, gifts, feedback messages, notification token and performance data) and the data on your device is cleared. Your name is no longer shown in the Hall of Fame. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete your account page.

To ask us to delete your data, email ugur.enes.ustundag@gmail.com with the subject "Pixel Parade data deletion request" and include your player ID from the Game's Settings screen (tap Copy next to "ID"). You can also open the email from the Game with Settings → Contact Us.

Within 30 days of your request we will:

  • delete your guest game account on our server, together with your profile, cloud save, Arena, League and Leaderboard entries, gifts, feedback messages, notification token, performance data and the link to your Play Games or Game Center player ID;
  • request deletion of the analytics and crash data linked to your player ID from Google Firebase.

We will confirm by email when this is done. After deletion your progress cannot be restored. Purchase records kept by Google Play or the Apple App Store are managed by them under their own policies, and we may keep records we are legally required to keep. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game.

You can also contact us at the same address to ask what data we hold about you, or to correct it.

Settings → Request My Data gives you a copy of everything our server keeps about you, instantly. You can also email us.

9. How long we keep data

  • Analytics data in Firebase: up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports in Crashlytics: up to 90 days.
  • Your game account, profile, cloud save, feedback messages and performance data on our server: until you delete your account or ask us to delete them.
  • Monthly Leaderboard entries: for the current month; the top 3 of each month stay in the Hall of Fame until the account is deleted.
  • Notification token on our server: until you turn notifications off or delete your account.
  • Purchase check records (product, transaction ID, time): as long as needed for accounting and fraud prevention, or as required by law.
  • Data held by AdMob, Google Play and Apple: according to their own policies.

10. Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We process data to provide the Game you asked for (cloud save, online features, purchases), for our legitimate interests in keeping the Game stable, secure and well balanced (crash reports, analytics, performance data, fraud prevention), and, for personalized ads, on the basis of your consent. You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. They use safeguards required by law, such as standard contractual clauses.

12. Security

All data sent between the Game, our server and our service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to our server is limited to us.

13. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, contact us and we will delete the related data.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will be announced in the Game or on its store page.
